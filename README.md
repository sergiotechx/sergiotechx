## 👋 About me · Sobre mí

**EN** — I'm a blockchain business developer and Web3 builder based in Medellín. I design and ship smart contracts and dApps across several ecosystems, and I teach others to do the same through workshops and bootcamps.

**ES** — Soy desarrollador de negocio blockchain y Web3 en Medellín. Diseño y publico smart contracts y dApps en varios ecosistemas, y enseño a otros a hacerlo mediante talleres y bootcamps.

- 🔭 Building: certificates & verification (**Qedify**), agrotech financing (**AgroFactoring**), tokens in Rust (**NexoToken**)
- 🎓 Teaching: Soroban / Stellar, Solana + Anchor, Sui, Aptos, Monad
- 🌱 Always learning new chains, in English and Spanish
